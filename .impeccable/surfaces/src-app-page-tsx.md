# Surface: Writing desk (home `/`)

Mode: Operate. Audience: B2 students at UTP drafting the Week 4 response paragraph on laptop or phone. Task: write (part by part or paste a full draft), get rubric feedback, revise, compare drafts. Constraint: the AI coaches, never rewrites. Related surfaces (examples, expressions, rubrics) inherit the same world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The draft lives on a squared notebook sheet and comes back marked like a teacher marks it. Refuses the editor-plus-AI-sidebar SaaS default.
OWN-WORLD: White 5 mm squared paper (faint blue-grey grid) with a red margin rule; ballpoint navy ink #1C2533; four highlighters (topic blue, summary green, reaction orange, conclusion lilac) as translucent swipes; red-pen #C0392B for circles, ticks, score. Tabular figures. No texture, no handwriting fonts, no page curl.
STORY: Student sees one sheet, writes into it, sees each part highlighted, the word track fill, then asks for marking and gets margin notes plus a circled score; past drafts stack as numbered sheets.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Sheet fills width; left margin holds 01–04 part keys and the word track; top right the red-circled score; mode switch (Part by part / Paste full) sits on the sheet header. Primary action "Mark my paragraph" pinned at the sheet foot.
FORM: Graph-paper notebook, list position 4, seed 10913c07.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
